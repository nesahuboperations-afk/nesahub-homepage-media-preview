STAGE-1 PRICING

Agree the scope.
Understand the cost.

Service fee / commission

NESAHub earns a small commission on completed jobs (commonly 5%–10%) depending on the category and job size. This helps us run verification, support, and tracking.

Fair costing model

We aim to reduce bargaining by using standard job categories, typical cost ranges, and clear material quotations. Basic jobs can use minimum price guides; complex jobs use inspection or assessment.

Client confirms scope and price first. Work begins after agreement. Receipts can be recorded. Ask NESAHub to explain the applicable costs for your request.
